## How to read a REBUILD

A fabricating layer's code produces numbers from the simulator's world or from nothing. Rebuilding one means: the real sensor's driver, the layer's own physics written against the no-fabrication contract (`upin/core/no_fabrication.py`: decline and say why when the input is missing), a simulation that speaks the sensor's own protocol, tests of its declines and its stated limits, and a mission-catalogue entry. The honest layers built so far (146 to 154) are the pattern; each took one commit.
