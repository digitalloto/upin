### Conditions that must hold

Even with every sensor attached, this layer declines unless:

- crumbs dropped while the fix was trusted and tight
- three or more crumbs in radio range (plus altitude), or four

### Notes

Never drops on an untrusted or loose fix. Stamp sigma = fix sigma and wind sigma over the fall. Ground-level range, line of sight.
