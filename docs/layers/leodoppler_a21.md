### Conditions that must hold

Even with every sensor attached, this layer declines unless:

- a pass of at least MIN_PASS_S with MIN_POINTS observations
- maximum elevation at least MIN_MAX_ELEV_DEG
- orbit no older than MAX_TLE_AGE_DAYS

### Notes

One coarse fix per pass on a band GNSS jammers do not touch. The mirror position across the track also fits; the prior decides.

## How to run it

This layer takes real input through its own methods and never invents a reading:

- `set_orbit()`
- `feed_doppler()`
- `set_prior()`
- `set_altitude()`
- `set_motion()`

Call those methods from a driver or the application. With nothing supplied it declines and names what is missing.
