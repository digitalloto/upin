### Conditions that must hold

Even with every sensor attached, this layer declines unless:

- model trained on the same airframe
- a learned wind (the drone flew steady legs on a few headings while GNSS was good)

### Notes

Velocity only, never a position. Flags conditions outside its training. Off in the box unless a model is configured.
