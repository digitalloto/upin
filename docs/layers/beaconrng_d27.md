## Sensors and data it needs

| three or more beacons that know their own positions | tripod, mast, tethered or free balloon; each carries its own GNSS or is tracked from surveyed ground sites | same radio module, a position source, a battery | — | no |

### Live inputs

| Input | Units | Supplied via | Why |

| shared secret | bytes | `set_secret()` | hop schedule and message signatures |
| beacon messages | id, time, position, sigma, seq, channel, signature | `feed_message()` | — |
| ranges | m, 1-sigma m | `feed_range()` | — |
| barometric altitude (optional) | m MSL, 1-sigma m | `set_altitude()` | lets three beacons make a fix |

### Conditions that must hold

Even with every sensor attached, this layer declines unless:

- three beacons with fresh signed messages and ranges, or four without altitude
- geometry with HDOP below MAX_HDOP

### Notes

Carrier-agnostic: whatever holds the beacon up. A beacon's own position sigma propagates into the fix. Band and power limits are the operator's responsibility.
