### Conditions that must hold

Even with every sensor attached, this layer declines unless:

- two independent observations (two bearings, or a bearing and a range)
- a cut angle of at least 15 degrees between bearings
- observations no older than 5 seconds

### Notes

Needs no radio of any kind, so it cannot be jammed or spoofed by RF. Forging it would mean physically moving a landmark.
